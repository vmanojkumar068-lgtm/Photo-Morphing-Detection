A morph combines characteristics from two or more faces to create one convincing new identity.

The anatomy of a morph

The image may look whole. Its evidence isn’t.

Morphing software finds shared landmarks — eyes, nose, jawline — then warps and blends the source images into a new composite. The result can fool people and some systems, but it often leaves a pattern.

Identity fraud

A single image can resemble more than one person.

False evidence

Altered imagery can change the context of a claim.

One questionable asset can cast doubt on a whole record.
